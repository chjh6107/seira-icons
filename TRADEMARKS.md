# Trademarks

This package ships 93 brand logo icons (`logo-*`), ported from the
[Ionicons](https://github.com/ionic-team/ionicons) icon set.

## The short version

The MIT license covers the **SVG artwork**. It does not — and cannot — grant any
trademark rights. Brand names, logos, and marks remain the property of their
respective owners.

- **No affiliation.** Inclusion of a logo does not imply affiliation with,
  sponsorship by, or endorsement from any brand. This project is independent and
  unofficial, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ionic (Drifty Co.).
- **Identification only.** Logos are provided so developers can identify and
  link to the services they integrate with — a nominative use.
- **Your use is your responsibility.** Trademark law turns on *how* a mark is
  used, not on where the file came from. Before shipping a brand logo, check
  that brand's own trademark and brand usage guidelines.

## Using brand logos responsibly

Do:

- Use a logo to identify or link to that brand's own product or service.
- Keep the logo's proportions and colors as the brand specifies.

Don't:

- Use someone else's logo as your own logo, favicon, or social/OG image.
- Imply a partnership you don't have — including "Trusted by", "Powered by", or
  customer-logo walls, unless you have permission.
- Modify, recolor, or recombine a mark in ways the brand's guidelines forbid.
- Use a logo in a way that suggests the brand endorses your product.

## Removal requests

If you own a brand and want its logo removed from this project, we will act on
it — no lawyer required, no justification demanded.

**Open an issue:**
[Trademark removal request](https://github.com/chjh6107/seira-icons/issues/new?labels=trademark-removal&template=trademark-removal.yml)

Please include the brand, the icon name(s), and how we can verify you represent
the owner. We aim to respond quickly and will remove the artwork on a good-faith
request, without requiring a formal legal demand.
